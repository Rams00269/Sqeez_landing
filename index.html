Guests bid into windows. Not into your tee sheet.

You set the windows, the price floor and who can ask. Live tee times are never shown to guests.

  1. A guest names a price

    They pick a window you opened and offer an amount on top of the green fee. They can save a card so a no-show costs them, not you.

  2. You say yes, or your rules do

    Offers below your floor go to the waitlist. Offers above your auto-approve line are accepted for you. The ones in between wait for you.

  3. They are in

    The guest hears straight away. The booking lands in your own system, and you can see where it came from.

You set the line. Squeez keeps to it.

  • Prices are per person, and only on the amount above your green fee.
  • Your auto-approve line is never shown to guests.
  • Some days you want to decide everything. Switch auto-approve off and every offer waits for you.

Guests find you where they already are.

One set of rules. Every place a guest can book follows it.

Your own booking page
On your domain, with your name on it. Guests pay in dollars.
Loyalty programme sites
Members spend miles or points at your course. The programme never sets your price.
The Squeez apps
Explore on the web, and the mobile app, for guests who browse before they book.
Your hotel concierge
Concierges book for their guests from time you set aside for them.

The rest of the job, handled.

  • WaitlistGuests who offer less than your floor are kept, in order, and told the moment a time opens.
  • Split the costOne person books. Friends pay their share from a link, with no account.
  • No-show feesCharge the whole party, or only the players who did not turn up.
  • ChatbotAnswers guests from your own website and documents, so fewer questions become phone calls.
  • Every transaction, one listBookings from all your sources in one place, with the fees broken out.
  • Yield suggestionsSqueez suggests prices and holds. You approve them, or set limits and let it run.
